# Clinic local SEO

Apply this skill to the public clinic site and its local-search footprint. Search visibility is earned over time and cannot be guaranteed.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the business facts in the repository or an authoritative owner-provided source before changing them: business name, address, phone, hours, services, insurance, clinician credentials, and destination URLs.
2. Inspect the page's existing title, description, canonical URL, robots directives, internal links, sitemap entry, and structured data before adding anything. Avoid duplicate or conflicting markup.
3. Write for patients first: clearly explain the service, location, clinician, practical next step, and limitations. Avoid repetitive keyword stuffing, thin doorway pages, or unsupported medical promises.
4. Keep the name, address, phone, hours, and website consistent across the site and Google Business Profile. Flag an outdated GBP booking URL rather than changing a profile the current account does not manage.
5. Use only structured data that matches visible, current page content. Dentist/LocalBusiness markup can clarify entity details; it does not guarantee a ranking or rich result. Do not publish aggregate ratings or review data unless it is verifiable and compliant with Google's policies.
6. Verify crawl paths and sitemap in Search Console when authorized. Record whether a URL is indexed separately from whether it ranks or receives clicks.
7. Follow the project's branch → preview → review → main workflow for code changes. Explain dependencies on owner access to Google Business Profile or Search Console.

## Measurement

- Separate branded searches, local service queries, and informational blog queries.
- Track impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position by query and page; compare equivalent date ranges.
- Interpret local results in light of searcher location and Google's relevance, distance, and prominence signals.
- Treat ranking as an outcome to measure, never as a promised deliverable.
- Request only honest, voluntary patient feedback; do not buy, incentivize, or fabricate reviews.
